## Animation Specifications

### Timing
- Fast animations: 200-300ms (buttons, inputs, small elements)
- Medium animations: 300-400ms (cards, containers)
- Slow animations: 400-500ms (page transitions, large elements)

### Easing
- Default: easeInOut for smooth, natural motion
- Spring: For playful, bouncy effects (icons, stats cards)
- Linear: For continuous animations (spinners, gradients)

### Delays
- Staggered animations: 50-100ms between items
- Card sequences: 100-200ms between cards
- Page elements: 100-300ms for hierarchy

### Performance
- All animations use hardware-accelerated properties (transform, opacity)
- No layout-triggering animations (width, height, top, left)
- Framer Motion for complex animations
- CSS transitions for simple hover effects
