Introduction
The planned project is called AI-Powered Virtual Medical Assistant and Smart Doctor Calling Agent, the focus of which is to establish a set of artificial intelligence that will facilitate and enhance the process of communication with patients and doctors. Numerous patients find it difficult to explain their symptoms or the specialist to see. We are launching our system, which involves a chatbot that engages with patients in a conversational manner, studies their symptoms with the help of AI, and recommends appropriate doctors, depending on the conditions and the location that they report. The system will also include a calling agent that is specific to doctors and will handle calls from patients, appointments, and even inquiries automatically, thus eliminating the administrative burden on medical personnel.
The solution will be developed on Next.js as a front-end interface, Express.js as a backend API service, and PostgreSQL as a database manager. Workflow automation and integration between system modules will be done through n8n; the conversational chatbot will be powered by the OpenAI API. VAPI will be used in automating the doctor-side call management to facilitate a smooth process in voice-based communication.
Objective
The main goal of the project is to deploy a smart, AI-based medical assistant, which, having learned about the symptoms of the patient, will be able to give appropriate medical advice, will be able to recommend doctors nearby, and will be able to manage doctor calls automatically. The system is supposed to improve the availability, precision, and effectiveness of healthcare communication.

Patients will report their symptoms in natural language, and they will be analyzed with a developed and trained machine learning model that was created with the help of healthcare data, including the publicly available Kaggle Disease Symptom Prediction dataset. Express.js backend will be used to facilitate communication between the chatbot and the database, as well as automation services. The records of all the interactions and outcomes of the patient will be registered in a PostgreSQL database to keep them safe and analyze them further. 
In the case of doctors, the smart calling agent with VAPI will automatically control the incoming calls of patients. It will also give voice-based replies in case of frequent questions, handle the booking of appointments, and receive messages in case the doctor is not available.

Project Scope
The system will address AI-based interaction with patients, their symptom analysis, and doctor recommendations, as well as the automation of calls on the doctor's side. The system also assumes that users have access to the internet and gives the correct information regarding their symptoms. This project does not involve integration with hospital management systems or real-time medical device monitoring; however, it can be considered in case of expansion.
Feasibility Study
The project is highly feasible due to the availability of reliable APIs and frameworks.
Risks Involved: The main risks include the accuracy of the analysis of AI symptoms and the safety of sensitive patient information. These issues will be reduced with the help of the careful selection of the dataset, the ethical processing of data, and the safe design of the database.
Resource Requirement: The necessary assets are the development machines, access to OpenAI and VAPI API, an n8n instance to automate all these, and PostgreSQL cloud hosting. The schedule of the project fits the academic schedule quite well, and the experience of working in the team guarantees the timely performance. 
Solution Application Areas
This project has great potential in telemedicine, online consultation sites, and hospital support systems. Clinics can use it to automate their appointment management, and health organizations can use it to help patients engage in preliminary self-assessment. It can also be useful to NGOs and government initiatives to refer patients to the right medical care in places with poor healthcare facilities.
Tools/Technology
The proposed system will be based on Next.js as the frontend, Express.js as the backend, PostgreSQL as the database management tool, OpenAI APIs as conversational intelligence, Python for the ML model-training, VAPI as the call management tool, and n8n as the workflow automation and integration tool. The tools that will be used in the development will be VS Code, Docker, and Postman to test and deploy the API.
